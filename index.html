Brainrot - Privacy Policy

100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Brainrot ("we", "our", or "us") provides a personal digital wellbeing and reel counter application designed to help users combat doomscrolling. Your privacy is paramount: Brainrot operates 100% locally on your device with no external cloud servers and no data tracking.

1. On-Device Storage Only

All app statistics (reels tracked, time wasted counters, dopamine velocity metrics, and user preferences) are stored strictly inside your phone's private local SQLite database (brainrot.db). None of this data is ever transmitted, backed up to external servers, or shared with third parties.

2. Android Accessibility Service API Prominent Disclosure

Brainrot requires the Android AccessibilityService API solely to deliver its core functionality:

  • Targeted Application Detection: The service evaluates on-screen UI components in Instagram and YouTube to identify when a Reel or Short is in view.
  • Usage & Limit Enforcement: The service logs the view count locally to help you abide by your self-imposed scroll limits and returns you to your home screen when your limit is reached.

Strict Privacy Guarantee: The Accessibility Service does NOT monitor personal messages, passwords, keystrokes, personal accounts, audio, or financial transactions. No accessibility data is ever stored permanently or transmitted off your device.

3. Overlay Permission

Brainrot uses the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission to display an optional floating pill counter over video apps so you remain conscious of your screen time while browsing.
